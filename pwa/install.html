TallyPrime Assistant

Scan to install on your phone

Install on iPhone / iPad

1
Open the Camera app and point it at the QR code above. Tap the yellow notification banner that appears.
2
Safari opens the chatbot. Wait for it to fully load.
3
Tap the Share icon at the bottom (the box with the upward arrow).
4
Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
5
Tap Add in the top-right. The Tally AI icon appears on your home screen — tap it any time to launch.

Install on Android

1
Open the Camera app (or any QR scanner) and point it at the QR code. Tap the link that appears.
2
Chrome opens the chatbot. Wait for it to load.
3
Tap the menu (three dots ⋮ at the top-right of Chrome).
4
Tap Install app or Add to Home screen (wording varies by Chrome version).
5
Confirm. The Tally AI icon appears on your home screen and behaves like any other native app.
